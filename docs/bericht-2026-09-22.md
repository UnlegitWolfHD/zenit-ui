## Reviewer findings that changed the code

- Tooltip `aria-describedby` token logic froze the tab when the attribute was rewritten while the panel stood (Fable, measured 100 000 observer callbacks); fixed with a no-write-when-unchanged guard and a Chromium test that fails fast.
- The scroll listener of menu and tooltip reacted to every scroller in the document; a live console log closed every menu (Fable). Now only a scroller that contains the trigger counts.
- A disabled `a[zMenuItem routerLink]` still navigated (Fable). Capture-phase click on the menu.
- `.z-legacy` exclusion on the universal selector cost +24 % on a full style recalc; a split form brings it to +14–18 % (about 0.7 ms on a 4 000-node page), accepted and documented.
- `leiheAttribut` gave back the value at borrow time, not the caller's latest (the same bug the button had); fixed for all five call sites at once.
- `ZTheme` overwrote pre-set `data-theme`/`data-accent` with `storageKey: null` (a flash, reported by the frontend); now adopts them.
